When do you prefer using AI vs. searching Google?
I prefer AI for quick explanations, summaries, or coding help, and Google when I need reliable sources, documentation, or to verify information.

How do you decide when to ask a colleague instead?
I ask a colleague when I’m stuck after trying solutions, need context-specific guidance, or when the issue involves team systems or decisions.

What challenges do developers face when troubleshooting alone?
They can waste time, miss simple solutions, feel stuck, and lack different perspectives that could quickly solve the problem.
